## Authoritative Citations
1. **City of Boston — City Archives Explainer** (*100 Years Ago Today: Molasses Crashes Through Boston's North End*, January 15, 2019):

2. **Boston Public Library — Archival History Post** (*Boston's Great Molasses Flood*, January 7, 2019):
   - URL: `https://www.bpl.org/blogs/post/bostons-great-molasses-flood/`
   - Verified facts: Owned by United States Industrial Alcohol Co. (USIA) for industrial alcohol/munitions production; over **2.3 million gallons**; surge ~**35 mph** smashed buildings and damaged/wrecked elevated railway supports/structures; **21 killed**, approximately **150 injured**; court-appointed auditor attributed failure primarily to severe structural inadequacy rather than outside interference.
3. **History.com — Historical Overview** (*The Great Molasses Flood of 1919*):
   - URL: `https://www.history.com/articles/the-great-molasses-flood-of-1919`
   - Verified facts: 529 Commercial Street installation rushed in 1915 during WWI; prior reports of groaning/leaking seams; **21 fatalities** and roughly **150 injuries** from crushed structures, swept debris, and suffocation in cold viscous syrup.
