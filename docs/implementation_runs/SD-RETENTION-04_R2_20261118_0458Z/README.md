## 4. 10-Point Human Visual & Audio Review Checklist

Before approving any video for external release, a human reviewer must watch `preview_1080.mp4` and `final_4k.mp4` on both desktop and a 360px phone viewport and verify:

1. **0–3s Opening Hook Impact**: Confirm London's noon blackness/bus lanterns (`NOON LOOKED / LIKE MIDNIGHT!`) and Mount St. Helens' north flank bulge (`THE MOUNTAIN / WAS BULGING!`) communicate the mystery clearly without audio.
2. **Smoke / Plume Naturalness**: Inspect `final_4k.mp4` across smoke, lateral blast, and Plinian ash shots for hard quad borders, sorting pop artifacts, or low-poly blob shapes.
3. **No Clipped or Cluttered Overlays**: Verify every headline, callout card, and caption stays clear of YouTube Shorts top/bottom chrome and side edges on 360px width (especially Tacoma `shot_04` & Boston `shot_07`).
4. **Pointer & Target Alignment**: Confirm arrows (`~230 SQ MI ZONE`, `M5.1 FLANK SLIDE`, `TRAPPED BY WARM AIR!`, etc.) land cleanly on the intended physical feature during camera motion without occluding subtitles or faces.
5. **Cutaway Visual Polish**: Review Mount St. Helens `shot_01` cryptodome cutaway magma dome and London `shot_02` brick chimney cutaway to decide whether simplified geometric magma shapes need bespoke artist refinement.
6. **Continuous Visual Flow & Pacing**: Confirm zero dead holds or jarring whip cuts break comprehension across beats (including Mount St. Helens `shot_02/03` landslide and `shot_06/07` Plinian column pairs flagged by advisory `scene_diversity` WARN).
7. **Aftermath Clarity**: Confirm London's sunlit vs smoky 1956 Clean Air transition and St. Helens' dashed pre-1980 peak contour clearly convey the factual conclusion without misrepresenting scale.
8. **Voice Intelligibility & Pacing**: Listen with speakers & earbuds to verify SAPI narration clarity, pronunciation (`Mount St. Helens`, `Plinian`, `1952`, `1956`), and natural pause cadence against visual cuts (consider neural TTS upgrade before public release).
9. **Music & Volcanic/Ambient SFX Balance**: Check that procedural rumble, landslide roar, lateral shockwave, Plinian ash roar, and underscoring sit cleanly behind narration without masking consonants (`-15.0` to `-15.5 LUFS`).
10. **Factual & Historical Integrity**: Cross-check captions, callouts, and [historical_claims.json](historical_claims.json) / [factual_sources.md](factual_sources.md) against primary UK Met Office and USGS sources.
